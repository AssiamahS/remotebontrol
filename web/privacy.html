Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

The short version

Remote is a TV remote that runs entirely on your phone and your Wi-Fi. It has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. We never see anything you do with it.

1. Who we are

Remote is published by Sylvester Assiamah. Reach us through GitHub issues.

2. Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our systems.

3. What stays on your phone

The list of TVs you added (name, model, IP address, MAC address) and the pairing token each TV issued. This lives in the app's own storage on your device and is deleted with the app.

4. Local network permission

iOS asks for Local Network access so the app can find and talk to TVs on your Wi-Fi. Traffic goes only between your phone and your TV.

5. Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Updates to this policy will be posted at this address with a new date.
